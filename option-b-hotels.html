📝 Important Notes

Prices are estimates based on 2025/2026 travel blog data and booking sites. Confirm directly with accommodation before booking. Beach fale prices are often negotiable on arrival, especially in quieter periods.

  • Beach fales are traditional Samoan open-sided shelters — expect mattresses, mosquito nets, and ocean breezes rather than air conditioning and minibars.
  • Meals included is a huge perk — there are very few restaurants in rural Savai'i, so your accommodation is often your only dining option.
  • Booking ahead is essential for Falealupo (only 5 fales) and advisable everywhere in peak season.
  • All prices shown in AUD. Exchange rate fluctuations may affect final prices.
