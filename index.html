About this map

The background map (coast, roads, buildings, villages, shops, churches, water) is built into this page, so it works with no mobile data.

On the website, tap ⬇ Save offline to download one self-contained file to your phone, then open it from Downloads.

📍 button: tap once to show & follow your GPS position, again to stop. Dragging the map pauses following; tap to re-centre.

Top right: switch between 📴 Offline map and 🌐 Online map (needs internet). Your choice is remembered.
