🍔 Food & Shopping Guide

What's available on Savai'i?

  • Every village has small convenience stores with: chips, sweet biscuits, two-minute noodles, canned goods, rice, bread (sometimes), soft drinks, water.
  • Bread & cheese: Harder to find. Best bought at Salelologa Market on Day 2 after the ferry.
  • Fresh fruit: Roadside stalls sell bananas, pineapples, coconuts, pawpaw.
  • Salelologa Market: Best food shopping on the island — fruit, baked goods, fried chicken, cheap meals.

🎒 Quick Packing Reminders

  • Cash: Bring AUD/NZD to exchange, or withdraw SAT from Salelologa ATM. Budget ~SAT$500-700 pp for the island.
  • Snorkel gear: Bring your own for best quality. Hire is hit-or-miss.
  • Torch/headlamp: Essential for cave visits. Provided ones are weak.
  • Reef-safe sunscreen: Hard to find good SPF in Samoa. Bring from home.
  • Quick-dry clothes: It's 30°C and humid. You WILL sweat.
  • Lavalava (sarong): Cover knees in villages. Buy one at Salelologa Market.
  • Dry bags/ziplock bags: For phone, cash, camera in panniers. Rain comes fast.
  • Water bottles: 2 per person minimum. Outdoor Samoa provides small ones but bring your own big ones.
  • Insect repellent: Mosquitoes at dusk in the fales.
