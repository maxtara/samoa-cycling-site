🍽 Meals & tips

  • Breakfast: included at Samoa Beach Resort and Va-i-Moana.
  • Dinner: likely included at Tailua (Mon). Ask at Se'eti and Satuiatua. Jet Over / Salelologa has restaurants and shops.
  • Lunch on the road: village shops (bread, crackers, tinned fish, drinks). Thin on Tue west of Sasina — carry snacks.
  • Water: 2 L+ each, refill at every stay.
  • Cash: small notes (WST) for village fees and fales. ATM in Salelologa.
  • Ride early: it's hot by midday. Sunset ~18:25.
  • Dress: cover shoulders/knees in villages. Sunday is quiet — no swimming near villages.
